# How AI Is Reshaping Workplace Design
**Rapid Evidence Review & Workplace Strategy Recommendations | October 2026**

## Overview
An independent research project examining how AI is changing employee work patterns and what these changes mean for workplace design, space utilization, and employee experience.

The project synthesizes academic and industry research to develop evidence-based workplace strategy recommendations.

## Key Findings
- **Changing work patterns:** AI adoption is associated with changes in employee collaboration, learning, and individual work.
- **Workplace design:** Organizations increasingly expect AI to influence space planning and demand for flexible workspaces.
- **Employee experience:** Effective workplace design must balance collaboration, focused work, and employee needs.

## Strategic Recommendations
- Assess employee needs through surveys, interviews, and focus groups.
- Pilot flexible workspaces before large-scale redesigns.
- Combine employee feedback with occupancy and utilization data to inform workplace planning.
